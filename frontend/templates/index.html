Why EchoRisk AI?

AI-POWERED DIGITAL EXPOSURE AND CYBER RISK ANALYSIS PLATFORM

Billions of credentials and personal identifiers circulate on the dark web and hacker forums due to third-party data breaches. When services you use suffer a breach, attackers often execute credential-stuffing attacks against other websites you frequent.

Privacy-First Design

EchoRisk AI does not store scanned email addresses in any database or file logs. Searches are processed entirely in memory.

Responsible AI Reasoning

EchoRisk AI is restricted strictly to analyzing verified breach facts. It never fabricates breach claims or generates attack methods.

Actionable Guidance

Clear, prioritized defensive countermeasures help non-technical users protect their accounts immediately.
